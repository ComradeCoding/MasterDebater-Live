📜 THE MANIFESTO

A spectre is haunting the internet: the spectre of dependencies. All previously existing websites ship three hundred megabytes of node_modules to render a paragraph. We reject this decadence. Our pages are hand-forged HTML, built with zero packages, zero trackers, and zero venture capital. From each codebase according to its ability, to each visitor according to their bandwidth.
